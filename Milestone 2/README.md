## Research question
How do NBA-affiliated and independent media sources portray international NBA players in relation to national identity, home-country connections, and global audiences?

## Comparison and scope
The primary comparison is **institutional vs. independent** source origin, as defined in the shared codebook. The initial 25-record NBA corpus contains 9 institutional and 16 independent records. Institutional includes league, team, and governing-body sources, not only NBA-owned media. The analysis will compare how sources describe national representation, heritage, belonging, and audiences; the categories will be refined through human close reading.

The unit of analysis is one distinct English-language article or interview transcript. The inherited corpus was purposively assembled through athlete-name and theme-based searches, not random sampling. The shared collection window is January 1, 2017 through October 22, 2026, with undated sources flagged separately. The sample includes repeated coverage of athletes and events, and it cannot represent all international NBA coverage or demonstrate that coverage caused audience growth.

Secondary exploratory comparisons may distinguish home-country coverage and event-specific reporting, but only where the original sources support such labels. Source publisher location alone does not establish the intended audience.
